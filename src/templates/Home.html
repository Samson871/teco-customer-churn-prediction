Why Predict Customer Churn?

Customer churn prediction helps businesses identify which customers are likely to leave, allowing proactive measures to retain them. By analyzing customer behavior, service usage, and payment history, companies can increase customer satisfaction and boost revenue. Our tool leverages machine learning models to give accurate predictions that support strategic decision-making.
